## Trivial contributions

Typo fixes, one-line changes, obvious corrections, and documentation tweaks do
**not** require a signed CLA. Copyright does not meaningfully attach to work of
that size, and requiring a legal signature to fix a comment would be
performative. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Don't want to sign?

That's fine and it is not held against you. **Open an issue describing the
problem instead of a PR containing the fix.** A clear, reproducible bug report is
often more useful than a patch, and it carries no legal surface at all.
